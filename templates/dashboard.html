For educational purposes only. This tool provides a forensic estimate of Patent Term Adjustment (PTA) under 35 U.S.C. 154(b). It is not a legal determination and does not substitute for official USPTO records or qualified patent counsel.
⚠️
PTA applies only to utility and plant patents filed on or after May 29, 2000 under 35 U.S.C. 154(b). Design patents are excluded. Results depend on the accuracy and completeness of USPTO PAIR data.
🔬
Calculated PTA may differ from USPTO official PTA due to internal mailing rules, weekends/holidays, PTA petitions, or additional 37 CFR 1.704(c) categories not captured in public data. Always verify with the official patent record.
💡
Maintenance fees are due at 3.5, 7.5, and 11.5 years after grant for utility patents. Failure to pay results in patent expiration. A 6-month grace period applies with surcharge.
